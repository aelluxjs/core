# Optional PWA Extension — implementation and validation

Date: 2026-10-23. Branch: `update/beta.10`. Package: `0.1.0-beta.10`.

The PWA implementation is ready for review. Its focused checks pass, but the repository-wide release gates are **not all green**. The failures below must not be represented as a successful full validation or a release approval.

| Area | Files / result |

## Existing or unresolved repository-wide failures

The three unit failures occur in `tests/unit/layout-scheduler.test.mjs` at the severe-pressure, idle-gap and timer-recovery cases. Copies of the exact scheduler source and tests from `git show HEAD:<path>` reproduced the same three failures independently of the PWA work.

Ajax Href's minified Modern and Legacy artifacts are byte-identical to `HEAD`: 7,343 raw / 3,097 gzip bytes and 14,943 raw / 5,595 gzip bytes. They already exceed the existing raw limits (7 KiB / 14.5 KiB); Modern also exceeds its 3 KiB gzip limit. No global limit was increased to hide this.

Thirty browser failures were in `present-aria.spec.mjs`: five behavior/ARIA expectations and five stub-based cases across three engines. Two representative cases were independently reproduced in Chromium by routing every distribution request to its original `HEAD` bytes: the native animation-start ARIA expectation and `AelluxJs.onEvent is not a function` in the direct-pop stub case.

Four further failures occurred under the six-worker broad run: Chromium Scrollbox pointerup ordering, Firefox horizontal scrollbar drag, WebKit Overflow Marquee invisible-label timing and WebKit Present progress timing. These are separate from PWA; their individual rerun results are recorded below. They are not classified as confirmed PWA regressions or universally pre-existing defects.

All four cases passed when rerun across all three engines with three workers: **12 passed**. The failures were not reproduced in that focused rerun; the complete broad run nevertheless remains a failed gate.
